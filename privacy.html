Privacy Policy – The Slow Hearth

Last updated: 26 September 2026

What this application is

The Slow Hearth uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel "The Slow Hearth" to upload and schedule that channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data accessed

With the channel owner's consent, the tool uses the scopes youtube.upload and youtube only to:

  • upload videos, titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails to the owner's channel;
  • schedule publication times and add videos to the owner's playlists;
  • read the channel name to confirm which channel is linked.

Storage and sharing

  • The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never transmitted anywhere except to Google's servers.
  • No YouTube data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No analytics, cookies or tracking are used by this tool or this website.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party access.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the channel's About page.
